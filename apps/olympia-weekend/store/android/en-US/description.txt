The pocket program for Olympia weekend in Las Vegas. Everything happening from Wednesday through Sunday — the schedule, the five venues, the 154-booth expo floor, and the meet-and-greet times — all in one place, all offline inside the Palms.

Built for the fan who is actually at the venue, not the fan browsing history. If you want to know when doors open, which venue pre-judging is at, where the free pop-up gym is, or which booth Chris Bumstead is signing at today, this is the app. No account. No login. No ads. No subscription.

What the app does

• Now — one glance answers "what should I be watching right now" and "what's up next". The clock runs on Las Vegas local time regardless of where your phone thinks it is. All-day events surface on their day so nothing gets buried.

• Schedule — every event Wed through Sun with doors time, running order, venue, and access level (free, ticketed, or VIP). Filter by day, access, or venue. Tap any event for directions to the door.

• Expo — all 154 exhibitors at the World Fitness Expo in the LVCC South Hall. Filter by category — supplements, apparel, equipment, coaching, more. Fri and Sat stage-event times. Floor plan bundled so you can find booth B4 in the aisle, not on Instagram.

• Venues — five weekend venues on one map with drive times from the Palms and one-tap directions to Google Maps.

• Athletes — the roster across Men's Open, Classic Physique, 212, Men's Physique, Bikini, Wellness, Figure, Fitness, Women's Physique, Women's Bodybuilding, and Wheelchair. See each competitor's expo booth, meet-and-greet schedule, and Instagram. Tap "I saw this" when you spot them — the crowd confirmation count updates live.

• Event detail — three facts you need: when doors open, where the door is, and what ticket gets you in. Plus the running order, VIP photo opportunity times, and a Save button for your itinerary.

• Saved — bookmark from anywhere. Your saved list groups by day so you see Wednesday's plan, then Friday's, at a glance.

Made for the weekend. The whole app ships bundled so it works in the Palms elevator, the Convention Center basement, and on Vegas hotel Wi-Fi that keeps timing out.
