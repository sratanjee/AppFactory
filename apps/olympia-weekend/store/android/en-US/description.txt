Olympia Weekend is an unofficial fan guide for the 2026 Mr. Olympia in Las Vegas. It answers the one question every fan has at the venue: what is happening right now, what is next, and where do I go?

The app runs entirely on your device. No account, no login, no ads, no subscription. Everything you need ships bundled — the schedule still works in airplane mode inside the Palms.

What the app does

• Now screen — see what is happening at this moment, what is coming up next, and all-day events on one screen. The clock runs on Las Vegas local time regardless of where your phone thinks it is.

• Schedule — browse every event from Wednesday through Sunday. Filter by access level (free, ticketed, or VIP) or by venue. Tap any event to see doors time, running order, and directions.

• Event detail — the three facts you need: when doors open, where it is, and what ticket gets you in. Plus the full running order, VIP photo opportunity times, and a Save button for your itinerary.

• Athletes — find competitors across Men's Open, Classic Physique, Bikini, Figure, Wellness, 212, and more. See each athlete's expo booth, meet-and-greet schedule, and Instagram. Tap "I saw this" to confirm a sighting.

• Expo — browse all 150+ exhibitors at the World Fitness Expo. Filter by category. Find stage event times and the floor plan.

• Venues — all five weekend venues with drive times from the Palms. Tap any venue for turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps.

• Saved — bookmark any event. Your saved list groups by day so you can see your Wednesday plan, then Friday's, at a glance.
