Olympia Weekend is an unofficial fan guide for the 2026 Mr. Olympia in Las Vegas. It answers the one question every fan has at the venue: what is happening right now, what is next, and where do I go?

The app runs entirely on your device. No account, no login, no ads, no subscription. Everything you need ships bundled — the schedule still works in airplane mode inside the Palms.

What the app does

- Now screen: see what is happening at this moment, what is coming up next, and all-day events on a single glance screen. The clock runs on Las Vegas local time regardless of where your phone thinks it is.
- Schedule: browse every event from Wednesday through Sunday. Filter by access level (free, ticketed, or VIP) or by venue. Tap any event to see doors time, running order, and directions.
- Event detail: the three facts you need — when doors open, where it is, and what kind of ticket gets you in — plus the full running order, VIP photo opportunity times, and a Save button so you can build your personal itinerary.
- Athletes: find competitors across Men's Open, Classic Physique, Bikini, Figure, Wellness, 212, and more. See each athlete's expo booth, meet-and-greet schedule, and Instagram. Tap "I saw this" to confirm a sighting; the crowd confirmation count updates live.
- Expo: browse all 150+ exhibitors at the World Fitness Expo in the Convention Center. Filter by category — supplements, apparel, equipment, and more. Find floor plan and stage event times.
- Venues: see all five weekend venues on a map with drive times from the Palms. Tap any venue for directions — opens Apple Maps or Google Maps on iOS.
- Saved: bookmark events from anywhere in the app. Your saved list groups by day so you can see Tuesday's plan, then Friday's, without scrolling through the full schedule.
