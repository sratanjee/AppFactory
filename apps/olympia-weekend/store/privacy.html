Olympia Weekend

Privacy policy

Effective 22 September 2026

Olympia Weekend does not collect personal information. No name, email, phone number, or location is ever recorded or transmitted.

What stays on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted:

Data Where it lives What it is used for
Bookmarked events Device storage (SharedPreferences) Shows your saved events in the Saved view
First-touch source Device storage (SharedPreferences) Remembers how you first found the app (e.g., from an Instagram link) so Mixpanel can group usage by source — a single text string, not your identity
Install hint dismissed Device storage (SharedPreferences) Hides the "add to home screen" banner after you dismiss it

None of this data leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

Analytics — Mixpanel

The app uses Mixpanel to understand how fans use it during the weekend. This helps decide what to improve for future events.

What Mixpanel receives What it does not receive
Anonymous device ID (a random string, not your name or email) Your name, email, phone number, or any identifier that could be traced back to you
Aggregate usage events: which screens you viewed, which filters you used, which tabs you tapped Your location, contacts, photos, or microphone
Platform and app version IP address stored by us (Mixpanel may see it transiently at the network layer but we do not log it)
First-touch source (e.g., "instagram" or "direct") Any data from other apps on your device

Mixpanel's own privacy policy is at mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Crowd confirmations — Supabase

When you tap "I saw this" on an athlete's page, the app sends a confirmation to our database to help other fans see that an athlete is at a specific booth or location.

What is sent:

Field Value
Anonymous device ID A random string generated when you first open the app. Not linked to your name, account, or Apple/Google ID.
Appearance key A composite of athlete ID + date + start time + venue + booth. Identifies which sighting you are confirming, not who you are.
Timestamp When you tapped "I saw this".

Confirmations are rate-limited to 30 per device per hour to prevent abuse. No name, email, or phone number is ever associated with a confirmation. Supabase's privacy policy is at supabase.com/privacy.

Deep links

The app supports the olympia:// URL scheme. When you tap a link that starts with olympia://, your device opens the app and navigates to the relevant screen (for example, olympia://schedule opens the Schedule tab). No data about this navigation is transmitted to us beyond the Mixpanel screen-view event described above.

Directions

Tapping "Directions" on any venue or event opens Google Maps or Apple Maps with the venue address pre-filled. The address is hardcoded in the app. We do not receive or log your current location, your route, or any navigation data. Google Maps and Apple Maps have their own privacy policies that govern what they collect when you use their apps.

Third-party advertising

There are no ads in this app. We do not use any advertising SDKs. No data is sold or shared with advertising networks.

User accounts

There are no user accounts. There is no sign-up, no sign-in, and no profile. The app does not require an Apple ID, Google account, email address, or phone number at any point.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
