Price a job while you're standing in the customer's driveway, and send them a branded PDF before you drive off.

Wash Quote & Invoice is a pressure-washing quote and invoice tool for solo operators. It runs on your phone, works with no signal, and it does one thing well: quote the job, send the PDF, take a deposit, get to the next stop.

Quote from the driveway
Tap New quote from the driveway, snap a photo of the surface, pick a service from your price list, adjust the square footage, and you have a live total. Add a deposit percent and your pay-via text (Venmo, Zelle, Square, check — your call). Save it.

Your prices, your units
Set your own services once. House soft wash, driveway, roof, deck, fence, commercial flatwork — priced per square foot, per linear foot, per hour, or flat. Every quote pulls from your list.

Looks like a real company, because you are one
The PDF has your logo, your business name, itemized lines, the deposit line, your pay-via text, and before / after photo pages. It renders the same on every device, in iOS Files, Gmail, and WhatsApp.

Show the work, get paid faster
Attach before photos on the quote. Add after photos when the job is done and convert to an invoice with one tap. When it's time to collect the deposit, create a Stripe payment link straight from the invoice.

One price, unlimited documents
Business setup is free. Sending quotes and invoices needs Pro: $9.99 monthly, $59.99 annual (7-day free trial), or $79.99 one-time. No document caps. No branding on your PDFs but yours. No pushy upsell prompts later, because there is nothing left to upgrade to.

Local-first, private by default
Every quote and invoice lives on your phone. No accounts, no login, no cloud dashboard. Automatic backup to iCloud Drive or Google Drive. You can export every job as a PDF or the whole ledger as CSV at any time.

What it is not
Not scheduling, not routing, not team management, not accounting sync. If you want those, use Jobber. This is the tool for pricing a job in a driveway and sending a PDF before you leave it.

Subscription details
Auto-renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in App Store settings. The 7-day free trial applies to the annual plan.
